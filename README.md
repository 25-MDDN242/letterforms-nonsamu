Alphabet Name: Glorbo

My initial idea was to try stick to a triangular shape with the letters but after drawing my idea out in paint, I couldn't think of a way to do a few of the letters like k, r, and z. A few of the other letters also didnt really 'fit' visually alongside the other letters or if I did make them match the others, letters like A and O were nearly indistinguishable from each other so I moved onto trying a different approach. My new approach was using different sized circles and erasing them to make the letters. This is how I drew the letters on paint. When trying to do this using p5js, I realised that making a circle and then trying to cover up parts of it with the background color probably wasn't the best way of coding my idea. This is when I switched over to using arcs rather than ellipses after going through the different shapes in the p5js documentation. It was a lot easier to draw the shapes I was after and a lot easier to manipulate if I had any adjustments I wanted to make.

From the original idea drawn on paint, I kept a lot of the same ideas but a few of the letters were changed. These were:
B - added an arc on the left so that it was distinguishable from 8
G - made the second arc shorter because I thought it kept the overall circular shape better
I - I thought that the two arcs meeting at the ends made it look like a narrow O. Making the arcs touch in the middle, in my mind, made it look like the I had the upper and lower lines of a capital I without having to add extra arcs for it and keeping an overall 'straight' shape.
J - used a second arc to make it closer to a J
P - reversed the first curve to make it look nicer
Y - changed entirely because I wasn't happy with how it looked in the original idea. I took inspiration from doing T for it.

The inspiration for the more rounded shapes came from the Unown Pokemon as they have a variant for each letter. Even though some of them aren't specifically circle shaped, I liked the way that some of them like the H and M looked which was what helped develop my idea. 
https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/villains/images/7/79/Unown_Alphabet.png/revision/latest?cb=20180613225530 
The name of the font came from another material that inspired the circular shape which was the Italian brainrot character from recent internet memes, Glorbo Fruttodrillo which is a watermelon with a crocodile head and limbs.
https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/brainrotnew/images/7/72/Glorbofruttodrillo.jpeg/revision/latest/thumbnail/width/360/height/450?cb=20250328131308 

Since the design for these letters was very minimalist, I thought the best way to make them more interesting was to stick to something minimal but made them 'pop' or stand out. I accomplished this by adding layers underneath the letters to make them appear to glow. This made them stand out against the dark background and I liked the way the electric blue looked against the navy. I thought adding too much else would take away from the way the letters looked so I chose to stick with a simple glowing effect.
